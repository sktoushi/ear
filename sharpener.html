How scheduling, auto mode & infinite reset work
Each segment is a card. Grading runs SM-2 (Anki), updating ease factor, reps and interval; intervals are mapped to steps ahead in the queue, so Again/Hard resurface soon and Good/Easy get pushed back until they graduate. State is saved to IndexedDB keyed by video + segment length, so a reload resumes exactly where you left off.

The mastery signal blends learned fraction, mean ease factor, and recent retention. When it crosses the threshold (comprehension high), progress auto-resets — SRS state clears, the round counter increments, and drilling continues infinitely. Uncheck Infinite auto-reset to stop at completion instead.

Auto mode waits for the configured number of loop passes on the current segment, then rolls a grade from the chosen bias distribution and submits it, moving straight to the next card — hands-free, endlessly. A pending roll is cancelled the instant you press a grade key/button, pause playback (autopilot holds while paused), undo, or untick the box; in all those cases the original manual flow prevails. Autopilot grades still drive SM-2 (otherwise nothing would advance) but are excluded from the Retention stat and the mastery retention window, and each card shows a ⚙ pill counting how many of its reviews were machine-rolled.
